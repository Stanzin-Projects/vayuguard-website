About VayuGuard

Guarding you, guarding tomorrow

We are a Make-in-India climate-tech company on a mission to make every indoor space safer — through patented hybrid air purification engineered for Indian air.

Our Mission

Clean air is not a luxury.
It's infrastructure.

Founded in New Delhi — a city that breathes some of the world's most polluted air — VayuGuard Climate Tech designs and manufactures hybrid purification systems that treat air where people actually live and work: inside AHUs, ducts, split ACs, classrooms, wards and offices.

Our patented HCAC platform was developed with India's climate, dust loads and HVAC realities in mind — washable media, low pressure drop and purification that scales from a single bedroom to an entire hospital campus.

Our Journey

From a Delhi garage to cleaner air nationwide

  • 2019

    The founding insight

    Our founders — HVAC engineers — watched Delhi's air quality collapse every winter and asked: why treat rooms one by one when the problem flows through the ducts?

  • 2021

    HCAC is patented

    The Hybrid Central Air Cleaner is patented: electro-charged, washable media that cleans the entire HVAC supply path with minimal pressure drop.

  • 2023

    From HVAC to every breath

    VayuShield, UVGI, ANOP and the VayuView monitor round out a full-stack portfolio — whole-building to wearable.

  • 2026

    500+ installations

    Trusted by homes, offices, hospitals, schools and industry across Delhi NCR and India — with live IAQ monitoring in every deployment.
